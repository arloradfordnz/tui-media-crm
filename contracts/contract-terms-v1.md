# Contract terms, version 1 (draft for Arlo to read)

Written 24 November 2026. Not reviewed by a lawyer. Nothing here is live until Arlo approves it and it is wired into the CRM.

How a contract is put together:

- **Page 1, the Project Sheet.** Filled in by the CRM from the popup answers. Different for every client.
- **Page 2, the Terms.** The wording below, the same for every client. Three kinds of contract (Monthly, One-off project, Event or wedding) share most clauses. Clauses 4, 7 and 8 have a version per kind, and clause 9 has a business version and a private-person version.

`{braces}` are filled in by the CRM. Nothing else changes between clients.

## Page 2: Terms

### 1. The work (all contracts)

We make what the Project Sheet lists. Anything else is extra work, which we quote and you approve in writing before we start it. You give us access to the place, people and products we need to film. If you are late with access, feedback or payment, our dates move by the same amount.

### 2. Edits and approval (all contracts)

We deliver every edit through the client portal. Each video includes 2 rounds of changes. A round is one set of changes to the existing edit; a new idea or a reshoot is extra work. You have 2 working days to approve an edit or ask for changes. If we don't hear from you we send a reminder, and the edit counts as approved 2 working days after that.

### 3. Payment (all contracts)

You pay each invoice by bank transfer within 7 days of it being sent. We may pause work while an invoice is overdue. Costs beyond the fee are payable only if you agreed to them in writing first.

### 4. Shoots and cancelling

**4 (Monthly).** Either of us can move a shoot at no cost with 24 hours' notice. If we cancel a shoot, or weather or something outside both our control stops it, that month's unmade videos move to the next month, once. If you cancel with less than 24 hours' notice, don't turn up, or don't give us access, those videos lapse without refund, because we set the time aside for you.

**4 (One-off project).** Your deposit holds the shoot date. Either of us can move the shoot at no cost with 24 hours' notice. If you cancel the project, we keep the deposit, because we held the date and turned other work away, and you pay for any work already done beyond it. If we cancel a shoot, or weather or something outside both our control stops it, we rebook for the next date that suits us both. If we can't do the work at all, we refund everything you paid for work not done.

**4 (Event or wedding).** Your deposit holds the date. If you cancel, we keep the deposit, because we held the date and turned other work away. If the event is postponed, the deposit moves to the new date, once, if we are free that day; if we are not, or it moves again, that counts as you cancelling. If we cannot film on the day, we refund everything you paid. If weather or the venue limits filming, we film what we safely can and the fee stays payable.

### 5. Who owns what (all contracts)

Once you have paid in full for a video, you own the copyright in the final video. We keep the raw footage and will send it within 5 working days of you asking, once you have paid; you may use it for your own purposes. We may show the work in our portfolio and on our social media unless you ask us in writing not to. Licensed music and stock footage may be used only as part of the finished videos. You may edit the videos, and you don't need to credit us. We keep your files for 12 months after final delivery.

### 6. People and safety (all contracts)

You are responsible for telling the people we film at your place or event (staff, customers, guests) that filming is happening, and for getting any permission needed. We use footage of people only for this work and our portfolio. You tell us about any hazards on site, and we may stop filming if it is not safe.

### 7. Ending the agreement

**7 (Monthly).** Either of us can end this agreement by giving the other 14 days' notice by email. Either of us can end it sooner if the other seriously breaches it and does not fix that within 10 working days of being told in writing. You pay for work done up to the end date. If you have paid for videos we have not delivered, we will either deliver them within a reasonable time or refund you for them, and we will tell you which in writing.

**7 (One-off project, event or wedding).** This agreement ends when the work is delivered and paid for. Either of us can end it sooner if the other seriously breaches it and does not fix that within 10 working days of being told in writing; you then pay for work done to that point, and we refund anything paid beyond it, other than a deposit we keep under clause 4.

### 8. What we don't promise, and limits

We do not guarantee views, enquiries, sales or any other result. Neither of us is liable to the other for indirect loss or lost profits.

**8 (Monthly), add:** Each of us is liable to the other for no more than the fees paid in the 3 months before the claim.

**8 (One-off project, event or wedding), add:** Each of us is liable to the other for no more than the fee.

These limits do not apply where the law does not allow them.

### 9. Consumer law

**9 (Business client).** We are both in trade and you are buying these services for your business. We agree that the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 does not apply (section 43 of that Act), and that sections 9, 12A, 13 and 14(1) of the Fair Trading Act 1986 do not apply (sections 5C and 5D of that Act), and that this is fair and reasonable.

**9 (Private person, including weddings).** Nothing in this agreement limits your rights under the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993.

### 10. The rest (all contracts)

We each keep the other's private business information confidential. We are an independent contractor, not your employee. Neither of us is liable for delays caused by things outside our reasonable control. Changes to this agreement must be agreed in writing (email is fine). If there is a dispute we talk it through first, then go to mediation in Nelson. New Zealand law applies. This agreement can be signed electronically.

## Review of this draft

Read against the Fair Trading Act's unfair-terms test (a term must protect a real interest and rights should be roughly mutual).

**Mutual, so low risk:** moving a shoot (both sides, 24 hours), ending (both sides, 14 days), serious breach (both sides), liability cap (both sides), delays outside control (both sides).

**One-sided, with the reason written into the clause:**

- Keeping the deposit on cancellation. Defensible while the deposit is proportionate. The CRM should refuse a deposit over 50% of the fee. $500 on a wedding from $1,200 is fine.
- Lapsed videos on a late client cancellation (monthly). Balanced by the rollover when we cancel.
- Deemed approval after a reminder. Standard, and the client gets a reminder first.

**Decisions for Arlo (each is my choice, change any of them):**

1. **No replacement videographer (clause 4).** Arlo's call: if we can't film on the day, the client gets a full refund.
2. **"You pay for work already done beyond the deposit" (one-off, clause 4).** New. Covers a client who cancels after you have scripted or filmed more than the deposit is worth.
3. **Weather at an event: fee stays payable (clause 4).** You still turned up and filmed what you could.
4. **No illness or bereavement exception for weddings.** I left it out to keep the rule you asked for (cancel = deposit kept). The postponement rule already lets a couple move the date once. Add an exception if you would give the deposit back in that case anyway.
5. **Weddings: the couple owns the film (clause 5).** Same as every other contract, as you asked. Many wedding filmmakers keep copyright and give a personal-use licence instead. Yours is more generous and simpler.
6. **Portfolio opt-out is "ask us in writing".** A wedding couple can opt out the same way a business can.
7. **Raw footage "for your own purposes".** For a business that means its marketing; for a couple, personal use.

**Dropped from the old AI-written contracts:** the separate privacy and health and safety sentences are merged into clause 6, and the "no GST" line lives on the Project Sheet only.

**Length:** an assembled page 2 is about 560 words for monthly, 600 for a one-off, 610 for an event. That fits one A4 page at the current contract type size, so the whole contract stays at 2 pages.

**Not checked by me:** that the portal stores a timestamp and the exact signed text for each signature, and section numbers against the current Acts online (they match the brief already in `lib/contract-drafting.ts`).
